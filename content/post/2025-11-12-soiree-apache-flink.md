### [Apache Flink for Java Developers](https://speaking.gamov.io/mrxzS1/stream-processing-as-youve-never-seen-before-seriously-apache-flink-for-java-developers) - par Viktor Gamov

Stream Processing has evolved quickly in a short time: only a few years ago, it was mostly simple real-time aggregations with limited throughput and consistency.

Today, many stream processing applications have sophisticated business logic, strict correctness guarantees, high performance, low latency, and maintain terabytes of state without databases.

Stream processing frameworks also abstract a lot of the low-level details away, such as routing the data streams, taking care of concurrent executions, and handling various failure scenarios while ensuring correctness.

During this talk, I will also try to mention flink agents (a new flink module, announced just a few months ago). So you will get a glimpse of agents as well.

À propos de Viktor :
